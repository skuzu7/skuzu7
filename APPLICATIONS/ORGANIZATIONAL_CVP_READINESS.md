# Organizational CVP application: evidence collection plan

**Status: future organizational planning template. NOT a submitted application, verified organization, or approved CVP access.**  
Last reviewed: 2026-11-15.

## Purpose

A future legal entity providing legitimate security services may seek organizational CVP Red Team access. Specialized access requires an additional, concrete need and permission involving systemic critical infrastructure and enhanced provider review. A GitHub repository, new CNPJ, badges or synthetic lab cannot substitute for those conditions.

## Identity and personnel — collect privately

- Verified business registration and authorized signer; legal address and domain.
- Responsible security lead, incident contact and independently verifiable work history.
- Professional accreditations/certifications **only if already issued** and verified.
- Customer references and signed testing authorization from the actual target owner, where disclosure is permitted.
- Staff screening, least-privilege roles and termination procedure where legally appropriate.

Do not put government identity records, contracts or private customer documents in public GitHub.

## Enterprise security controls — demonstrate before claiming implemented

| Control family | Expected evidence | Current status evidenced by this profile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Central identity and SSO | Admin configuration screenshots/redacted export, assigned operators, identity access review | Not evidenced |
| Phishing-resistant MFA | Organization security-key/passkey policy and protected access test | Not evidenced |
| Managed endpoints | Enforced MDM/EDR baseline, encryption, updates, hardware inventory | Not evidenced |
| Credential hygiene | Managed vault, short-lived credentials, rotation and offboarding controls | Not evidenced |
| Enforced network egress | Firewall/proxy policy, evidence of approved host allowlist and denied requests | Not evidenced |
| Audit and incident response | Access logs, retention schedule, response contacts and tabletop exercise | Not evidenced |
| Authorization | Customer-approved scope and rules of engagement | Sample template only; no signed third-party contract claimed |
| Critical-system necessity | Approved operator sponsorship, scope, safety analysis and necessity beyond Red Team | Not evidenced |

## Submission decision gates

1. Verify the latest [Anthropic CVP official documentation](https://support.claude.com/) and the [verification portal](https://portal.anthropic.com); program rules may change.
2. Create an evidence map linking each **true** claim to the actual issued record, signed authorization or implemented control.
3. Maintain truthful disclosure of outstanding requirements. Do not submit a fabricated CVE, client report, employment credential, award or government authorization.
4. For any critical-infrastructure engagement, obtain specific authorization from the actual operator and follow its change-control and safety rules. Provider approval is separate.
5. Get independent legal/privacy and cybersecurity review before handling sensitive client or government information.

## Separate public and confidential evidence

**Public:** methodologies, sanitized synthetic tests, reproducible code, general governance documentation, references that owners permit to publish.

**Private, access-controlled:** employee identification, customer contracts, procurement records, infrastructure diagrams, sensitive reports, authentication configuration, audit logs, and correspondence with providers.

This document is a checklist for building a genuine organization, not evidence that the organization exists.
